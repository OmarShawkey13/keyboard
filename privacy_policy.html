Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to RCKeyBoard, developed by Omar Shawkey. Your privacy is our top priority.

Privacy Commitment: RCKeyBoard does not collect, store, or transmit your private typing data, passwords, or credit card information. All typing intelligence happens locally on your device.

1. Information Handling

Our application is built to function with maximum security:

  • Local Storage: Your personal dictionary and learned words are stored only on your device. We never upload them to any servers.
  • No Tracking: We do not track your location, contacts, or any personal identifiers.
  • Sensitive Fields: The keyboard automatically disables learning and suggestions when you are in a password input field.

2. Permissions

We only ask for essential permissions:

  • Vibration: To provide haptic feedback.
  • Internet: For optional online translation and emoji asset management.
