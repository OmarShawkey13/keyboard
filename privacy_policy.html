Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

Welcome to RCKeyBoard. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we handle information when you use our application.

Important: RCKeyBoard is designed with a "Privacy First" approach. We do not collect, store, or transmit your private typing data, passwords, or credit card information.

1. Information Collection

Unlike other keyboard apps, RCKeyBoard works primarily offline to ensure your security:

  • Personal Data: We do not collect any personally identifiable information (PII).
  • Typing Data: Your dictionary and learned words are stored locally on your device only. We do not have access to what you type.
  • Sensitive Info: Our app is programmed to stop learning/suggestions automatically when you type in password fields.

2. Permissions Used

RCKeyBoard requires the following permissions to function correctly:

  • Vibrate: Used to provide haptic feedback when you press a key.
  • Internet: Used exclusively for the optional online translation feature and downloading emoji assets.
  • Network State: Used to check if the translation feature is available.

3. Third-Party Services

We may use Google Play Services for app stability and crash reporting. These services may collect information used to identify your device for debugging purposes only.

4. Security

The security of your data is important to us. Since your typing history is stored locally on your device, it is protected by your Android system's security features. We recommend keeping your device updated for maximum security.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
