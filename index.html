Privacy Policy

Commitment to User Data Protection

This Privacy Policy describes how RCKeyBoard (the "Application"), developed by Omar Shawkey ("Developer", "We", "Us"), handles your data. We take your privacy seriously and have engineered this app to be private by design.

Typing Data Never Collected
Cloud Storage 100% Local
Passwords Strictly Private

1. Zero-Collection Philosophy

RCKeyBoard is a local-first application. We believe your typing habits are your business. We do not monitor, record, or transmit the text you type via the keyboard. This includes messages, notes, emails, and any other text input.

2. Local Dictionary & Learning

To improve your typing experience, the app learns your common words and suggests them:

  • On-Device Only: The "Learned Dictionary" and predictive text data are stored exclusively in the Application's private storage on your physical device.
  • No Syncing: Your personal dictionary is never synced to any cloud service, ensuring it remains under your total control.
  • Sensitive Field Detection: The Application detects "Password" and "Credit Card" fields. In these instances, all learning and suggestion features are automatically disabled to prevent sensitive data from being cached.
Android Warning: When enabling RCKeyBoard, Android shows a standard warning about keyboards collecting text. This is a system-wide notice. We guarantee that RCKeyBoard does NOT collect or abuse this access.

3. Permissions & Usage

The Application requests only the minimum required permissions:

  • Vibrate: To provide tactile haptic feedback for a better typing experience.
  • Internet Access: Required only for the optional "Online Translation" feature (using Google/Microsoft APIs) and for downloading high-quality emoji assets. No typing history is ever sent to our servers.
  • Network State: To ensure features like translation only attempt to work when a connection is available.

4. Third-Party Services

The Application may use limited third-party services for specific features:

  • Google Play Services: For crash reporting and analytics to improve app stability.
  • Translation APIs: If you use the translation tool, the specific text you wish to translate is sent to the translation provider. This is only done at your explicit command.

5. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
