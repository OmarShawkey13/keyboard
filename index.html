Official Privacy Policy

RCKeyBoard

Last Updated: May 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how RCKeyBoard, developed by Omar Shawkey, handles your data. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of user privacy through a Local-First architecture.

Typing Data Never Collected
Cloud Sync Disabled (Off-line)
User Media Private URIs

1. Privacy by Design

RCKeyBoard is engineered to be a standalone tool. We do not track, monitor, or transmit any text you type. This includes personal messages, passwords, or emails. All data stays on your device.

2. Technical Feature Transparency

  • Intelligent Learning: Your personalized dictionary and bigram prediction data are stored in a secure SQLite/Room database located in the app's private storage. No cloud synchronization is utilized.
  • Theming & Custom Backgrounds: When you personalize your keyboard using a custom image, the Application uses standard Android content URIs to load the image locally. No personal media is ever uploaded.
  • Clipboard Management: The app locally caches copied text for your convenience. This history is stored in-memory or in local-only cache and can be cleared at any time.
  • Secure Field Detection: The keyboard automatically identifies sensitive input fields (Passwords/Credit Cards) to suspend all learning and suggestions, ensuring no sensitive data is cached.
Developer Guarantee: While Android displays a standard warning about third-party keyboards, RCKeyBoard officially guarantees that it does not use its access to collect or exploit your data.

3. Permissions & Usage

  • Vibrate: For tactile haptic feedback on key presses.
  • Internet Access: Used exclusively for the optional Online Translation feature (at your request) and fetching high-quality Emoji assets. No typing logs are sent.
  • Microphone: Used only when the Voice Typing feature is triggered to utilize the system's speech-to-text engine.
