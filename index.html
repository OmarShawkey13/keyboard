Privacy Policy

Commitment to User Data Protection & Transparency

This Privacy Policy describes how RCKeyBoard (the "Application"), developed by Omar Shawkey ("Developer", "We", "Us"), handles your data. Our keyboard is engineered with a privacy-first architecture, ensuring that your personal data never leaves your device unless explicitly requested by you.

Typing Data 100% Private
Images/Themes Local Storage
Clipboard On-Device Only

1. Zero-Collection Philosophy

RCKeyBoard does not monitor, record, or transmit your keystrokes to any remote server. Your messages, passwords, and notes remain strictly on your physical device. We do not have access to what you type.

2. Smart Features & Local Storage

  • Learned Dictionary: The app learns your typing style to provide better suggestions. This data is stored in a local SQLite/Room database and is never synced to the cloud.
  • Custom Themes: If you use a personal image for your keyboard background, the app accesses your media via secure Android URIs. We do not upload your photos to any server.
  • Clipboard Manager: The app locally caches recently copied text to allow quick pasting. You can clear this history at any time, and it is never shared externally.
  • Sensitive Fields: RCKeyBoard automatically detects password and credit card fields to disable learning and suggestions, preventing sensitive data caching.
Standard Android Warning: Android displays a default warning when you enable any third-party keyboard. We officially confirm that RCKeyBoard does not utilize its access to collect or store your private information.

3. Permissions & Why We Need Them

  • Internet Access: Required only for the Live Translation tool (sending specific text to translation providers at your request) and downloading Emoji assets.
  • Vibrate & Audio: To provide tactile haptic feedback and audible key-press sounds.
  • Media/Storage: Used exclusively to let you pick and apply custom background images for your keyboard themes.
  • Microphone: Used only when you trigger the Voice Typing feature to hand over input to the system's voice engine.

4. Third-Party Integration

We use limited third-party services to ensure stability and functionality:

  • Translation APIs: When you use the translation feature, the specific text is sent to the provider.
  • Google Play Services: Used for basic crash analytics to fix bugs and improve performance.

5. Data Deletion

You have full control. You can reset your learned dictionary, clear your custom themes, and wipe all application data at any time from the app settings or Android system settings.
